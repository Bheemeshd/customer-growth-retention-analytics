# Model card: six-month churn prioritization

## Intended use

Rank synthetic customers by the likelihood of placing no order in the six months after 2025-06-30. The output supports audience sizing and randomized retention-test design.

## Not intended for

- automated adverse decisions or differential service levels
- individual-level certainty claims
- estimating whether a retention offer will change behavior
- use on real people without privacy, legal, fairness, and model-risk review

## Inputs

Recency, frequency, net revenue, average order value, margin rate, tenure, and acquisition cost. Protected demographics are neither generated nor used. Email consent is deliberately excluded from training and used only as an activation gate.

## Algorithm and validation

The repository implements logistic regression directly in NumPy with standardized numeric features. A seeded 70/30 random split produces a held-out AUC of 0.722; the top 20% of held-out scores captures 31.9% of observed churners. These values describe one synthetic dataset and are regenerated by the pipeline.

## Known risks

- Random splitting can overstate performance when customer behavior drifts over time.
- AUC measures ranking, not calibration or economic impact.
- Acquisition cost can proxy for channel strategy and may change structurally.
- Churn labels depend on the selected six-month window and purchasing cadence.
- Targeting high-risk customers without a control group creates selection bias.

## Required production controls

Temporal validation, probability calibration, subgroup error analysis, drift monitoring, data minimization, consent enforcement, contact-frequency rules, analyst override, campaign holdouts, and documented ownership.
