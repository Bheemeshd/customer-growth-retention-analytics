# Business brief

## Scenario

The CRM team has a broad win-back program but no shared view of customer value, churn risk, or acquisition quality. Blanket discounting is expensive and makes campaign measurement difficult.

## Stakeholders

- CRM / Lifecycle Marketing: eligible test audiences and contact strategy
- Growth: acquisition source quality and payback
- Finance: margin and CAC assumptions
- Analytics / Data Science: label, validation, governance, and measurement

## Deliverable

Create a reproducible customer mart and dashboard that joins segmentation, cohort trends, value, model ranking, and experiment-ready action groups while keeping the analytical caveats visible.

## Success criteria

- No future information enters model features.
- Test performance is reported on held-out customers.
- Consent is required for priority activation audiences.
- Model scores and campaign treatment effects remain conceptually separate.
